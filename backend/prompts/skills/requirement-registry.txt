Treat functional_requirements and non_functional_requirements as one canonical
Requirement Registry. Compare entries by actor, trigger, action, governed object,
observable outcome, failure behavior, and quantitative constraints rather than by
ID or wording. Merge semantic duplicates and redirect all acceptance references to
the retained stable FR/NFR ID. Preserve separate entries only for distinct testable
obligations; expose conflicting overlaps instead of guessing. One requirement must
contain one obligation.
